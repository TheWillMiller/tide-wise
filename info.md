# TideWise

Tide predictions with optional fishing bite-window scoring for Home Assistant.

TideWise shows current tide height, next high/low tide, a 24-hour tide chart, optional safe-tide limits, moon phase, and fishing quality bands using configurable local weather and marine entities. NOAA CO-OPS is the default provider, with early Canada CHS/DFO support, UK support through the UKHO Tides Home Assistant integration, and international beta support through compatible Home Assistant tide sensors.
